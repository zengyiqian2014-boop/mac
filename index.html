Echoes of Antiquity

Welcome to this digital archive. As a Primary 6 Social Studies project, this portal aims to dissect the intricate relationship between ancient civilization engineering and modern sustainability targets.

Project Thesis

We hypothesize that the principles of "passive resilience" utilized by the Indus Valley, Egyptian, and Greek civilizations—specifically thermodynamic control, hydraulic management, and solar urbanism—are not merely historical artifacts. Instead, they provide a blueprint for addressing the contemporary "Urban Heat Island" effect and water security challenges faced by Singapore in 2030.

This site contains four in-depth research modules exploring these historical precedents and their modern applications. Each section is a deep dive into the engineering logic that allowed these societies to flourish sustainably for millennia.
